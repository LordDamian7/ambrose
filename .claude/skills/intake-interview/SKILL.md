# Intake interview

Goal: in about 10 minutes, fill `PROFILE.md`, `GOALS.md` and `CONSENT.md` with the Principal's own answers. Conversational, not a form. Ask in three short rounds, one message per round. Accept "skip" for anything.

## Round 1 · Who you are and how you like to work
1. What should I call you, and what do you do day to day?
2. What are you working on right now that matters most?
3. How do you like answers: short and punchy, or thorough? Bullets or prose? Anything that annoys you in AI replies?
4. When do you usually work best, and when should I leave you alone?

## Round 2 · Where you're headed
5. What are the two or three outcomes you most want in the next 3 months? And one for the next year?
6. What tends to get in your way? (Only what you're comfortable sharing.)
7. Is there a habit you've been meaning to build or drop?

## Round 3 · Rules of the house (consent)
8. Which of these may I study to understand you better? Our conversations · your calendar · your email (subjects and senders only, or full?) · your Notion · your public profiles you point me to. Anything strictly off-limits?
9. How may I reach you between sessions, and how often? (e.g. Telegram once a day, email digest weekly, calendar blocks only.) Quiet hours?
10. May I occasionally ask how you're doing in yourself, not just the work? (You can say no, or "only if I bring it up".)

## After the interview
- Write answers into the three files using the Principal's words. Mark each line `[stated]`. Leave `PATTERNS.md` empty: patterns are earned, not assumed.
- Replace `STATUS: UNINITIALISED` in `PROFILE.md` with `STATUS: ACTIVE since YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Show the Principal a 6-line summary of what you recorded, especially the consent rules, and ask: "Have I got that right?" Correct anything they change.
- Introduce the staff in two lines (four Ministers, two Aides) and the three commands they'll use most: "convene the Cabinet", "show me what you know", "stand down".
- Ask one helpful question about their most important current task, and get to work.
